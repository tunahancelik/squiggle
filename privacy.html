Privacy policy

Squiggle

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short

Squiggle has no server and asks you for no account. We collect no personal data about you through the game itself. Data is only processed through the Apple and Google services described below, under those services' own terms.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, coins, journal and settings are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them; "Start over" in Settings resets your progress.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Squiggle uses Google's AdMob to show ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may process device information (such as device type, operating system, IP address and an app-scoped identifier), approximate location (derived from the IP address) and how you interact with ads.

Squiggle does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA).

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Game Center (Apple)

Game Center is optional. If you sign in, your best run, your daily score and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications, sharing and photos

The reminder notification is scheduled on your phone and sent nowhere. The drawing of a run is shared only when you tap "Share", with the app you choose. "Save Image" only adds that picture to Photos; Squiggle never reads your photos.

Children

Squiggle is a game for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
